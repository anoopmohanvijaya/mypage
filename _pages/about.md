I am a **fluid dynamicist** with a PhD in Aerospace Engineering from IIT Madras, India and three years of postdoctoral research experience. My research interests are: turbulent jets, waves and instabilities in rotating and stratified fluids. I have expertise in experimental fluid dynamics as well as CFD simulations of applied fluid flows in HPC clusters. Recently, I have been exploring implementation of ML methods to parameterize sub-grid scale chaos using toy problems. 

I like to go places- explore in quiet ways, since I do not record my travels nor collect souvenirs. I am a weekend footballer, fortnightly biker and an occasional hiker. I am a tea person, interested in tea-chats on science, football, (motor)cycling and philosophy.
